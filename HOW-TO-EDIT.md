# How to edit your site

Everything on this site is yours to change. You don't need to know any code.

---

## Logging in

Go to **your-site-address/admin**

Click **Login with GitHub**. That's it. Bookmark this page.

---

## What you can change

The menu on the left has everything:

| Section | What it controls |
|---|---|
| **Blog Posts** | The posts on your Blog page |
| **Skin + Mind Articles** | The four article cards on the Skin + Mind page |
| **Interviews** | Your Expert Voices interviews |
| **Research Spotlights** | The study breakdowns on the Research page |
| **Research Library** | The list of sources at the bottom of the Research page |
| **Page Text** | Your Home page, About page, and site settings |

---

## Writing a blog post

1. Click **Blog Posts** → **New Blog Post**
2. Fill in the boxes:
   - **Title** — the headline
   - **Date** — today, usually
   - **Category** — pick one, it shows as a little label on the card
   - **Icon** — the small picture on the card
   - **Short description** — one or two sentences, this is what people read on the Blog page before they click
   - **Main photo** — optional, click to upload from your computer
   - **The article** — the actual writing
3. Click **Publish** at the top

Your post appears on the site in about a minute. Refresh the page if you don't see it.

---

## Writing the article itself

The big box works like a normal document. There's a toolbar for **bold**, *italic*, headings, quotes and lists.

**To link to a study:**

1. Select the words you want to be the link
2. Click the link button in the toolbar
3. Paste the study's web address

**To add a picture in the middle:**

Click the image button in the toolbar and upload it.

---

## Adding a source to the Research Library

1. **Research Library** → **New Source**
2. **Citation** — write it out: `Author. Title of study. Journal Name, year.`
3. **Link** — the web address of the study
4. **Topics** — tick every one that fits. This is what makes the filter buttons work.
5. **Publish**

---

## Adding a research spotlight

1. **Research Spotlights** → **New Spotlight**
2. Fill in the five boxes. They're the same five every time, so the page stays consistent:
   - Research Question
   - What researchers studied
   - What they found
   - Why it matters
   - Citation and link
3. **Publish**

---

## Adding an interview

1. **Interviews** → **New Interview**
2. Their name, job title, one short quote for the card, their photo
3. The full interview goes in the big box. Use a heading for each question and normal text for their answer.
4. **Publish**

The photos automatically alternate left and right down the page.

---

## Changing your Home or About page

**Page Text** → pick the page. Change the words, upload photos, click **Publish**.

On the About page you can add or remove whole sections with the **Add** button, and drag them to reorder.

---

## Changing your email address or site name

**Page Text** → **Site Settings**.

---

## Photos

Any **Image** box lets you upload straight from your computer or phone.

For the best results:
- Big photos at the top of a page: wide, roughly 1600 x 700
- Your portrait: tall, roughly 800 x 1000
- Interview headshots: square
- Card images: roughly 800 x 450

Try to keep each photo under about 400KB so the site loads fast on phones.

**Before you upload a photo of a person, make sure you have their permission**, and a parent's permission if they're under 18.

---

## Things to keep in mind

**You can't break the design.** The layout, colours and fonts are locked. Whatever you type gets styled automatically.

**Every article already has a disclaimer** at the bottom saying this isn't medical advice. You don't need to add one.

**Check your sources.** If you write about a study, add it to the Research Library too, so people can check your work. That's the thing that makes this site look serious.

**Saving without publishing:** click the dropdown next to Publish and choose **Save as draft**. It won't go live until you publish it.

---

## If something goes wrong

Nothing you do in here can permanently break anything. Every change is saved with a history, so it can always be undone.

If the site doesn't update after a few minutes, or something looks wrong, message Chloe.
